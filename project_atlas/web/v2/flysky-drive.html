Driving setup Read only. Set on the rover in config/atlas_flysky_drive.json and reviewed by the owner.

When FlySky driving stops the rover

  • Arm switch Driving needs the arm switch fully on. It must be seen off first, and the throttle must be centred when you arm.
  • Signal lost or receiver failsafe The rover stops at once and the E-STOP latches.
  • Stop switch Anything but the run position stops the rover and latches the E-STOP.
  • Xbox switched off The E-STOP latches, as it always has.
  • Receiver centre values A failsafe that only centres every channel cannot keep the rover armed.
