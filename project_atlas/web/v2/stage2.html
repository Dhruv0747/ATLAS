Master plan

Stage 2 · gentle left/right turns

  1. Park ATLAS on open floor (the hall is best): 1.5 m clear in front, 0.6 m clear on both sides, pointing along the free space.
  2. Manual mode. E-STOP released. Remote in your hand, sticks centred, hands off.
  3. Type the operator PIN and press START.
  4. ATLAS curves gently LEFT about 25° (about 30 cm), stops, then curves gently RIGHT about 25° and stops. Slow: 0.10 m/s.
  5. Press B on the remote at any time if anything looks wrong — it stops at once.

Run log
ATLAS checks each turn with the IM10A gyro and the LiDAR: the turn must go the commanded way, end by reaching the heading, stop cleanly, and left + right must cancel out. It stops by itself if anything comes within 0.30 m of the path, if a sensor goes stale or if it drives 0.6 m without turning. A PASS is recorded on the Master plan board and unlocks Stage 3.
