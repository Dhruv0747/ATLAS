Speed check (the rover drives ~0.6 m forward slowly by itself)

  1. Place the rover in open space: at least 1.5 m clear ahead, nothing within 0.6 m. Nobody standing in front.
  2. Keep the mode on Manual. Remote in hand, sticks centred, then release the E-STOP (press and release LB).
  3. Enter the PIN and press Speed check. The rover drives slowly and stops by itself. If anything looks wrong, press B at once.
  4. Bring the rover back and run it once more. 2 PASS = the speed blocker clears by itself.
